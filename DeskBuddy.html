DeskBuddy, a small robot that cleans your desk and talks back

A palm-sized desk robot: it vacuums crumbs and eraser dust, avoids falling off the edge, and answers when you say “hey desk buddy”. Designed end to end in Fusion, from air path to printable parts.

01 — The idea

A desk tool, not a toy

Desk vacuums exist but they are handheld and noisy. I wanted something that runs on its own while you work, and that people actually want on their desk — so it has a face and a voice.

The constraints were tight: everything had to fit in a 160 mm body powered by a single 18650 cell, and the robot must never drive off the table.

02 — Engineering

Air path, sensors and packaging

A 5015 blower pulls air through an intake under the body into a dust bin. Two N20 motors drive the wheels. Five VL6180X distance sensors watch the table edge. A XiaoZhi voice module handles the wake word and conversation.

03 — Where it stands

Designed, not built yet

The full assembly is done: 50 components, 66 bodies, 26 printable parts with drawings. Runtime is estimated at about 73 minutes from the power budget, above my 20–30 minute target.

No physical prototype has been tested yet. The next step is printing, wiring and measuring real suction and runtime.

What I take away

Packaging was the real design problem: every millimetre of air path competes with the battery, the wheels and the sensors.

What comes next

Print and assemble the first prototype.

Measure suction, noise and runtime against the estimates.

Tune the edge-detection thresholds on real desks.
