Formula Student Arts et Métiers · Team project

Keeping track of every part of a race car

In a team of about twenty students building a Formula Student car, I own the bill of materials and procurement: what we need, who supplies it, what is ordered and what is on the shelf.

01 — The problem

A race car is a few hundred parts and a deadline

Before the BOM, part lists lived in different files per sub-team. Nobody could say quickly what was missing, what was ordered, or what a change in one sub-system meant for the budget.

My job was to build one structured BOM for the whole vehicle and keep it alive through the season.

02 — What I did

One BOM, linked to suppliers and stock

  • Structured about 380 references by sub-system, with quantity, status and supplier.
  • Managed around 12 suppliers, including SKF, Igus, Drexler, Taylor Racing and Norelem.
  • Set up stock tracking so sub-teams know what is on the shelf before ordering.
  • Kept the BOM aligned with the CAD as the design changed.
